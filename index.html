In your headset

Point a controller at the brass ticket below you, then press the trigger. Squeeze either grip to bring the ticket in front of you. Choose Exit experience at the bottom of the ticket to leave VR and close the page.

Right stick: snap turn. Left stick forward: point at the water and release to move there. Travel always starts with you.

On this screen

Drag to look. Arrow keys to turn. Space to start or stop drifting. V changes viewpoint. R returns home. M mutes. Escape closes these controls.

Exit experience stops everything and closes this page. If your browser keeps the tab open, it will become a blank page.
