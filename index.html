More options & controls

On this screen

Drag to look. Space boards, departs or pauses. V changes seats; U climbs to the roof. H or Escape toggles this menu. T changes the time; R returns to the station; M mutes sound. Arrow keys turn the view.

In your headset

Squeeze either grip to open or close the menu. Point and press the trigger to choose. Right stick turns you. Push the left stick forward, point at the platform, the shore, the carriage floor or the water, and let go to step there. Look and lean naturally.

Choosing a seat pauses the journey. Opening this menu keeps your current journey and view.
